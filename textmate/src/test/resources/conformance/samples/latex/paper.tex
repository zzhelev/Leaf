\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
Three-way merges compare \emph{both} sides with their base, as in
\begin{equation}
  M = B + (O - B) + (T - B), \quad \text{for } |O \cap T| = 0.
\end{equation}
Inline math: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2$, see Section~\ref{sec:intro}.
